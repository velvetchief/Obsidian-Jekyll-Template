This past year, I ran into a bunch of friends whose kids were gearing up for college. Every single one of them was dealing with the same two headaches:

1. Dropping **$xx,xxx** on some "expert" consultant or counselor who did not help
2. Total confusion around **AI** - both picking the right school and figuring out career paths that won't get steamrolled by it.

One of my favorite episodes of _Inside The Foundry_ was the one with Siddharth Gupta. What stuck with me most was how fired up he was about actually guiding kids through this messy transition phase.

My take? Most **careers** are going to thrive in the AI era — as long as you treat AI as a serious multiplier. Notice I said careers, not jobs.

Lawyers who lean into AI will trample those who don't. Same for architects: the ones who use AI to design faster, smarter, and at higher quality will dominate. The ones who resist it?

This weekend I vibe-coded a quick 0-to-1 version of an **AI Skill Advisor** on Replit.

It's built to help high schoolers, fresh college grads, STEM post-grads, and working professionals start seriously leveling up their AI aptitude.

Right now it covers five core fields: **Engineering • Science • Arts & Design • Medicine • Business**

Check it out here: [Aptifier](https://aptfier.replit.app/)

It'll be open source in a day or two if anyone wants to poke around or contribute.

Meanwhile, ideas on how to make it sharper, more useful, or expand it are welcome :)
