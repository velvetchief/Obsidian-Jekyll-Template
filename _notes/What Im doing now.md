## Applied AI for small business

I run [KiloByte Collective](https://kilobytecollective.com/), an applied-AI lab for small business. We build the system, you test it against acceptance criteria written into the contract, and you own everything we ship.

[Dinealog](https://dinealog.com/) is the live one: it answers restaurant phones, all the calls at the same time, takes orders and books tables while the staff stays on the floor. I wrote about sitting in a restaurant watching it work a dinner rush in [[Dinealog]].
